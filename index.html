Questions

Frequently asked

Is Vyne free?

Yes. Vyne is free to use, with no ads right now. If that ever changes down the line, we'll be upfront about it, existing features won't quietly get paywalled.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Vyne works fully offline with zero sign-up. An account is only needed for the optional Cloud Backup, so you can restore your data on another device.

What happens to my data if I don't sign in?

It stays on your device, only. We never see it, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you turn on Cloud Backup yourself.

Can I delete my data later?

Yes, any time, right from inside the app, both your local data and any cloud backup. No waiting, no support ticket required.

Is there an iOS version?

Vyne is Android-first for now, with iOS on the way. Email us and we'll let you know the moment it's available.

How is this different from a regular to-do list app?

Flat lists flatten your life too: a work deadline sits right next to "buy milk" with no sense of scale. Vyne lets related things branch off each other, so each part of your life stays exactly as detailed, or as simple, as you want it.

Want in before it's public?

Vyne is Android-first, currently in closed testing ahead of a full launch. Email us and we'll add you to the list, or invite you to try it early.
