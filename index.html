What's inside

Everything your life needs, nothing it doesn't

No feeds, no likes, no ads to scroll past. Just your own structure, growing the way you do.

🔁

Habits that actually stick

Never-miss-twice warnings, identity-based prompts, habit stacking, and a spin-the-wheel picker for when you can't decide where to start.

🌿

A living structure

Branch anything as deep as you need: categories, projects, notes, due dates. Nothing gets flattened into one giant list.

📊

See your progress

Category breakdowns, 30-day habit consistency, streaks, and a due-date calendar, so you always know what's overdue, today, or coming up.

🔔

Reminders that respect you

At most one gentle check-in a day, at a time you pick. Due-date alerts and one-tap calendar export, per branch.

☁️

Your data, your choice

Vyne works fully offline. Turn on cloud backup if you want it; otherwise we never touch your data, and you can delete it any time.

🎨

Make it yours

Four themes, multiple profiles for different people or areas of life, and a personal avatar that's actually you.
